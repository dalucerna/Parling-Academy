Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

¿Cuántas clases incluye el plan mensual?▼
Todos los planes incluyen 8 clases al mes (12 horas). Plan Grupal: máximo 8 personas por grupo. Plan Personalizado: sesiones 100% adaptadas a tus objetivos más horas extras disponibles.
What level do I need to start?▼
No prior experience needed. We offer paths starting from absolute zero (A1) all the way to advanced C2. A short placement test ensures you join the right group.
¿Qué pasa si falto a una clase?▼
Si no puedes asistir a una clase, puedes reprogramarla poniéndote de acuerdo con el profesor o con el grupo.
Are the certificates officially recognized?▼
Our certificates are aligned with the CEFR international framework and recognized by universities, companies, and immigration bodies in over 180 countries.
How is Parling different from other apps?▼
Most apps focus on solo exercises. Parling combines live human interaction with 24/7 AI practice and real-world scenarios. You learn not just a language — you learn to use it.
